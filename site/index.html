01 · Reference

Drop in a track you love.

Alma listens to it bar by bar: tempo and key, the sections, how long the intro really is, where the kick leaves for the break and where it returns. That structure becomes the shape of your track.

02 · Sounds

Point it at your sample pack.

It picks a kit — kick, clap, hats, percussion, bass, synths, atmosphere — and shows every candidate it considered. Swap any sound. Audition it in place. Add a vocal if you have one.

03 · Direction

Style, length, tempo. Then listen.

Choose the style — Melodic Techno, Afro House, Tech House, eight in all — and turn the length to where you want it. Alma renders a mix of the whole track before anything is written, so you hear it first.

04 · Build

Press Build. Open Live.

Tracks, clips, section locators and sidechain — written into the empty Live Set in front of you in about a minute. Not a bounce: an arrangement you keep working on. Or export stems and MIDI for any other DAW.

What Alma does

  • Measures a reference the way a producer would: bars, sections, energy, kick in and out
  • Arranges your sounds with the rules we measured from 150+ techno tracks and 25 professional project files
  • Writes editable clips into Ableton Live, or stems + MIDI
  • Lets you hear the result before it writes anything

What Alma does not do

  • It does not generate audio. Every sound is yours
  • It does not compose melodies or chords. Bass and synth lines are placeholders in your key, meant to be replaced
  • It does not master, and it does not finish the track for you
  • It does not upload your sounds or your reference anywhere
